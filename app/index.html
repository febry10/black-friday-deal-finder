How this board works

Every deal on this page was found and checked on 27 November 2026 in a store listing or a deals roundup that names the store. Each card shows the product, the store, a link to the product at that store, the sale price, the usual price, the discount, and when and where the price was checked. The signal on each deal is our read of it: New low means the lowest price we have seen for it, Strong price a genuinely good cut, Routine sale an ordinary seasonal discount, and Inflated a sale price propped up by an inflated usual price.

Prices change fast during Black Friday weekend — always confirm the price at the store's checkout before you buy. Bargain Beacon is an independent deal finder and is not affiliated with any store named here.
